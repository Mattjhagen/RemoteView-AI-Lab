What is remote viewing?

It is the idea of describing a distant or hidden subject without ordinary clues. This lab lets you record and compare descriptions in a structured way. Similarities can happen by chance; results do not establish paranormal ability or AI consciousness.

A hit means the assigned scene was your first-choice match. Accuracy is the share of scored trials that were hits. With five pictures, a random first choice has a 20% chance of being the assigned scene.

What do these numbers mean?

Scored trials have submitted picture rankings. First-place accuracy counts only assigned scenes ranked number 1. Awaiting judging means a comparison hasn’t been submitted yet.

Completed AI trials have usable responses, but may still need judging. Invalid responses and failed runs are tracked separately and aren’t scored as misses. A dash means there is no scored estimate yet.
